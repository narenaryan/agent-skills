# Contingent package provenance and validation

Reviewed November 5, 2026. This extends existing whole-package negotiation guidance rather than adding a duplicate skill. The original skill's three source links remain unchanged. New companion references below are research inputs, not additions to monitored blog registries.

## Sources read

- [PON: Negotiator toolbox: Capitalize on differences](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/business-negotiations/negotiator-toolbox-capitalize-on-differences/). Displayed publication December 14, 2011; adapted from a December 2007 Negotiation newsletter item. Discovered November 2, 2026; fully retrieved and deeply reviewed November 5 after earlier retrieval limits. Historical backfill, not a new post. Supports using differing forecasts and inspecting private information, distorted incentives, ambiguous measurement and ongoing relationship costs. Its broad litigation and enforceability claims are not adopted as demonstrated results.
- [PON: Try a Contingent Contract if You Can't Agree on What Will Happen](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/dealmaking-daily/contingent-contract/), Katie Shonk. Page displays March 31, 2025; older comments indicate earlier history, so original first-publication date is unknown. Reviewed November 5, 2026. Supports separately eliciting future scenarios and checking whether counterpart information changes the apparent bargain. Willingness to accept is not treated as a truthfulness test.
- [Ian Larkin: The Cost of High-Powered Incentives: Employee Gaming in Enterprise Software Sales](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/13-073_cbb24c28-9e84-47d9-8a32-f01b73cfda13.pdf). HBS working paper 13-073, February 20, 2013; reviewed November 5, 2026. Its enterprise-software setting provides original evidence about incentive-related timing and discount behavior. The author explicitly limits causal identification absent exogenous incentive changes. This motivates inspecting actor-controlled timing/metrics, not a universal effect size, a causal claim about our workflow, or equivalence between employee commissions and buyer-facing terms. The later journal version was not the full text used here.

## Original synthesis and worked checks

The payoff table, probabilities, administration costs, budget/floor examples, quality-value example, sensitivity thresholds and unknown-evidence handling are original illustrative synthesis. The sources do not prescribe these numbers or establish an optimal package. The companion keeps state-by-state exposure separate from subjective expected values and commercial authority. Missing or disputed measurement remains unresolved rather than being assigned a payout.

Run from this directory:

```sh
python3 check_contingent.py
python3 -O check_contingent.py
```

Twelve original standard-library unittest methods passed in both modes on Python 3.12.14 on November 5, 2026. Exact Fraction arithmetic checks separate beliefs, administration costs, common-belief transfer accounting, seller downside, buyer maximum exposure, revised probability assumptions, indifference thresholds, incomplete evidence, invalid labels and probability endpoints. The quality counterexample illustrates why the same deadline label can hide different buyer value; no runtime test can establish that real measurement resists manipulation.

These are worked scenarios and arithmetic checks, not empirical sales-performance evidence. Probabilities and inputs are assumed rather than estimated; omitted taxes, timing, financing, overhead, legal costs and risk preferences can change a real comparison. There is no live buyer data, field experiment, causal benefit, approved pricing model, enforceability assessment or automated settlement authority. Real proposals need authorized commercial review and appropriate legal review; no proposal was sent or accepted. No software was installed and no third-party code was executed.
