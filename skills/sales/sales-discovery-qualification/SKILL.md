# Sales Discovery and Qualification

Qualification is decision-specific. Historical truth can outlive applicability.

## Establish evidence

Record claim, speaker, date, artifact, scope, and status: confirmed, inferred, unknown, contradicted, or needs-revalidation. Preserve history; CRM edits are not buyer evidence.

| Question | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Why change? | Incident, affected workflow, consequence, baseline |
| Why this solution? | Buyer-owned acceptance criteria and required proof |
| Who decides? | Authority for this expenditure, affected users, approval route |
| Why now? | Buyer deadline, delay cost, competing priorities |

Test account assumptions before pitching. Use SPIN flexibly: situation, problem, consequences, desired improvement. Quantify frequency, volume, unit cost, and period; label estimates and owners. Separate cash, capacity, and speculative revenue without double-counting. One person’s estimate is not organizational agreement.

## Revalidate selectively

Compare the current decision with the evidence's scope. This matrix is synthesis, not validated scoring.

| Change | Reopen affected claims |
|---|---|
| Larger spend, new business unit, tighter spending controls | Funding authority and approval conditions |
| Advocate changes role or leaves | Current influence, internal advocacy, stakeholder access |
| New requirements or deployment scope | Acceptance criteria, proof coverage, review prerequisites |
| New approval step or missed milestone | Dependencies, owners, achievable outcome date |

Mark affected claims needs-revalidation, not automatically contradicted; retain unaffected observations. Ask the buyer owner what remains applicable and resolves the gap. Reassess dependent dates using [mutual action plans](../sales-mutual-action-plans/SKILL.md).

Example: $20,000 pilot approval and passing sandbox tests do not establish authority or production clearance for a $200,000 cross-unit rollout. Preserve the pilot results; reopen funding and production-review claims. A new stakeholder alone does not invalidate every technical finding.

Recommend advance, investigate, defer, or decline. Advancement requires a useful buyer-agreed action, owner, and date; deferral requires a reopening condition. Unknowns are investigation targets, not invented win probabilities.

## Pitfalls

- Elapsed days alone establish neither expiry nor continued validity; honor explicit approval conditions.
- Enthusiasm and meetings do not prove buying progress; doing nothing can be rational.
- Revalidation is not permission to bypass contacts, contact buyers, update CRM, or bind either party.
